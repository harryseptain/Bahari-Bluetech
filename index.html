Who we are

Built from the
ocean up.

Bahari BlueTech is a marine engineering design and ocean technology firm headquartered in Mombasa, Kenya — operating at the intersection of precision engineering, ocean science, and blue economy development.

Africa's 38,000 km of coastline represents one of the planet's most underdeveloped frontiers — not for lack of resources, but for lack of locally owned technical institutions capable of leading the design, build, and governance chain. Bahari BlueTech exists to close that gap.

Our Story
Mission
To engineer Africa's blue economy from the ground up — designing and fabricating world-class marine infrastructure, advancing sustainable ocean resource management, and building the technical institutions that will define Africa's relationship with its oceans for generations to come.
Vision
A future where Africa leads the global ocean economy — where Kenyan engineers design the vessels, Kenyan scientists model the aquaculture systems, and African institutions govern the deep seabed with sovereignty, precision, and environmental stewardship.
Our commitment

Engineering with the
ocean, not against it.

Every system we design and every vessel we fabricate is evaluated against its ecological footprint. Sustainability is not an add-on — it is the engineering brief.

Read Our Commitment
🌊
Ocean Health
All vessel and aquaculture designs incorporate reef-safe materials, minimal-wake hull profiles, and low-emission propulsion specifications.
🐟
Fishery Stewardship
IMTA system designs are calibrated to reduce bycatch, eliminate monoculture dependencies, and restore depleted coastal fishery stocks.
⚡
Clean Propulsion
Diesel-hybrid and solar-assisted propulsion systems are standard in our vessel engineering specifications for inshore operations.
🌍
Local Economies
Designing for African operators means designing for local maintenance, local material sourcing, and coastal community livelihoods.
