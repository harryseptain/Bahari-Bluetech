The Untapped Potential of East Africa's Blue Economy
East Africa's Indian Ocean coastline holds over $300 billion in annual ocean economy potential — yet the region lacks the marine engineering institutions, vessel fabrication capacity, and governance frameworks to capture it. This in-depth analysis covers fisheries, IMTA aquaculture, ports, offshore energy, deep seabed minerals, and the structural changes needed to shift Africa from ocean consumer to ocean sovereign.
