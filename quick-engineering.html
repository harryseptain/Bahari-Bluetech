Simple process

How it works —
six steps.

We keep it simple. Send us what you have — even a rough photo or voice note — and we'll take it from there.

01
Send your sketch, photo, or idea

WhatsApp is fine. A rough photo, hand sketch, broken part, or short description. Don't overthink it — just send what you have.

02
We review and ask key questions

We look at what you sent and follow up with a few simple questions — dimensions, material, intended use, quantity needed.

03
You receive a clear quote and timeline

We give you a clear price and realistic turnaround time. No hidden charges, no vague estimates. You decide whether to proceed.

04
You pay a deposit

A deposit is required before we start. Payment is via M-Pesa or bank transfer. We confirm receipt and begin work.

05
We design, model, draw, or print

Our team works on your job. For most small jobs, you'll receive a progress update before final delivery. We keep you informed.

06
You receive your file, drawing, or prototype

Digital files (DXF, PDF, STL, STEP, DWG) are sent via WhatsApp or email. Physical 3D prints or models are available for pickup in Mombasa or can be arranged for delivery.

Before you contact us

What to send us.

The more you can share, the faster we can quote you. But don't worry — even a single photo is a good start. We'll ask for the rest.

  • A photo, sketch, or reference image of what you need
  • Approximate measurements or dimensions (rough is fine)
  • What the item should do — its function or purpose
  • Preferred material if you know it (plastic, metal, fiberglass, etc.)
  • Quantity needed (one piece or more?)
  • Your deadline — when do you need it?
  • Location — for physical delivery or pickup arrangements
  • Budget range if you have one (this helps us suggest the best option)

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I send just a photo or rough sketch?
Yes. A photo on WhatsApp is enough to start. We'll look at what you send and ask any questions we need. You don't need a formal technical drawing — that's our job.
Do you offer 3D printing?
Yes. We can 3D print parts, prototypes, models, and casings. We work with FDM (plastic) printing. Some specialist materials may require coordination with a printing partner. We'll let you know upfront.
How do you price 3D printed parts?
3D print pricing is based on print volume (size), material used, print time, and complexity. Send us the item you need and we'll give you a clear quote within 24 hours on working days.
Can you help students with project prototypes?
Absolutely. We work with university students, TVET learners, and school innovation projects. We offer CAD models, 3D printed prototypes, technical diagrams, and presentation renders. Rates for students are flexible — just tell us your situation.
Can you design parts for boats or fishing equipment?
Yes. This is one of our core areas. Boat deck layouts, fish boxes, bait tables, storage, fishing gear concepts, small marine accessories — all straightforward for us. Send a photo or description and we'll advise on what's possible.
Do you manufacture fiberglass products?
We design fiberglass product concepts and create technical drawings. Physical fiberglass fabrication requires a specialist GRP shop. We can advise on local fabricators who can build from our drawings.
Do you provide county building approvals or land survey services?
No. Quick Engineering Desk does not offer land surveying, civil engineering approvals, structural certification, or county permit services. If that's what you need, please consult a registered civil engineer or licensed land surveyor.
How fast can a small job be done?
Simple sketch-to-CAD jobs can often be turned around in 2–5 working days. 3D printed parts depend on print time and queue. Complex jobs take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline when we quote.
What file formats can I receive?
We can deliver PDF, DXF, DWG (2D drawings), STL, STEP, IGES (3D models), and JPEG/PNG renders. Just tell us what you need and we'll confirm if it's available for your job.
Can I request only a drawing without printing?
Yes. Many clients just need the drawing or 3D model to take to their own fabricator or printer. That's absolutely fine — you pay for the design work only, and we deliver the file.
