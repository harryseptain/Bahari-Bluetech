Frontier focus

Deep Sea Mining
Engineering & Governance.

The International Seabed Authority (ISA) is currently finalising the regulatory framework for deep sea mining — a global inflection point that will define who benefits from the polymetallic nodules, cobalt crusts, and seafloor massive sulphides that lie beneath international waters.

Bahari BlueTech holds deep technical expertise in ISA regulatory frameworks, nodule extraction engineering, environmental management plans for seabed disturbance, and the geopolitical dimensions of deep sea mineral governance as they pertain to African EEZ states.

We advise governments, development banks, and research institutions on deep sea mineral strategy — ensuring that African nations are not spectators but active participants in the governance of the seabed that lies within their extended continental shelves.
