How we engage

What kind of
partnership are you looking for?

Design Consultancy
Short-to-medium term engagements for vessel design, aquaculture layout, CAD modelling, and technical report production. Fixed-fee or day-rate basis.
Build Contract
End-to-end vessel fabrication from design through to launch — including regulatory submissions, stability testing, and operator handover training.
Advisory Retainer
Ongoing technical advisory support for government bodies, county administrations, and development organisations working on blue economy programmes.
Grant Co-Application
Joint applications to EU BlueInvest, UNDP, World Bank, and African development fund instruments — contributing technical capacity and documentation.
Research Partnership
Collaborative R&D with universities and oceanographic institutions — covering ROV development, IMTA system research, and deep sea mineral governance.
Impact Investment
We welcome conversations with impact investors interested in building Africa's marine engineering capacity. Our seed round opens 2026.
